    Where the numbers come from

    examples/plant-view.sh runs this command and puts the table that it prints into the page. A dark mark on the time line is an input of the operator, given with --set. A red mark is a result of the run. The fire starts small, because a cold furnace lights only a small part of the coal on the grate. The fire ends when the bunker is almost empty, because a law of the bunker then gives less coal.

    
    
    The page shows a temperature in °C and a power in MW. The run has them in K and W. Between two rows of the table, the page draws a straight line.
